About Super Tech Calibration Services

Where Every Measurement Meets Industrial Perfection.

Welcome to Super Tech Calibration Services, an ISO/IEC 17025 compliant calibration and testing organization headquartered at TC-622, Capital Highstreet, Bhiwadi, with state-of-the-art testing facilities at S-52, Phase III, RIICO Industrial Area.

We believe in more than just instrument calibration—our mission is to build lasting trust and complete compliance for manufacturing industries. Through accredited methodologies, master standards traceable to the National Physical Laboratory (NPL, New Delhi), and a dedicated team of B.Tech Mechanical and Electrical Engineers, we deliver dependable measurement solutions that guarantee smooth IATF 16949, ISO 9001, and OEM audits.

Comprehensive Capabilities

Our Calibration & Testing Disciplines

Full-spectrum metrology and environmental compliance services under one roof, backed by national standards traceability and certified master calibrators.

Discipline 01

Mechanical & Dimensional Calibration

High-precision dimensional calibration for tooling, machining, and automotive inspection tools with certified measurement uncertainties down to sub-micron accuracy.

  • Digital / Vernier Calipers (0-150mm, 200mm, 300mm, 600mm)
  • External, Internal, Depth & Blade Micrometers
  • Height Gauges, Plunger & Lever Dial Indicators
  • Granite Surface Plates (On-Site Flatness Mapping)
  • Plain Plug Gauges, Ring Gauges, Pin Gauges, Snap Gauges
Standard: IS 3651 / ISO 3611 View Full Scope
Discipline 02

Electro-Technical Calibration

Precision electrical parameter calibration for power generation, switchgear, panel builders, and electronics manufacturing using multi-product master calibrators.

  • Digital Multimeters (3.5, 4.5 & 6.5 Digit Precision)
  • Insulation Testers (Meggers up to 5kV / 10kV)
  • Earth Resistance Testers & Clamp-On Earth Testers
  • Timers, Digital Stopwatches, Tachometers & RPM Meters
  • AC/DC Clamp Meters, Power Supplies, Oscilloscopes
Standard: IEC 61010 / IS 11994 View Full Scope
Discipline 03

Thermal & Temperature Services

Comprehensive temperature profiling, sensor validation, and thermal uniformities for heat treatment, paint booths, cold storage, and chemical laboratories.

  • Industrial Ovens, Furnaces & Muffle Furnaces (to 1200°C)
  • Multi-Point Spatial Temperature Uniformity Profiling
  • Temperature Controllers, Indicators, Scanners & Recorders
  • RTD Pt100 Sensors & Thermocouples (J, K, R, S, T, B)
  • Incubators, Freezers (-80°C), Oil Baths, Water Baths
Standard: ITS-90 / ASTM E220 View Full Scope
Discipline 04

Pressure & Vacuum Services

Hydraulic, pneumatic, and vacuum calibration spanning from ultra-low draft pressure to high-pressure industrial hydraulic systems with deadweight master testers.

  • Analog Dial Pressure Gauges (-1 bar to 1000 bar)
  • Digital Pressure Gauges & Pressure Calibrators
  • Pressure & Differential Pressure (DP) Transmitters (4-20mA)
  • Low Pressure & Draft Gauges, Magnehelic Gauges
  • Pressure Switches, Safety Relief Valves, Manometers
Standard: IS 3624 / EN 837-1 View Full Scope
Discipline 05

Mass & Volume Calibration

Traceable mass metrology for laboratory balances, industrial platform scales, standard weights, and precision volumetric glassware for pharma and quality labs.

  • Analytical Balances (0.1 mg / 0.01 mg Micro Balances)
  • Industrial Platform Scales, Crane Scales & Weighbridges
  • Standard Calibration Weights (Class E2, F1, F2, M1)
  • Volumetric Flasks, Measuring Cylinders, Pipettes, Burettes
  • Variable Micro-pipettes & Bottle-Top Dispensers
Standard: OIML R 76-1 / ISO 8655 View Full Scope
Discipline 06

Environmental Testing Services

Comprehensive environmental laboratory testing supporting manufacturing plants, smelters, and industrial units with statutory pollution control compliance.

  • Ambient Air Quality Monitoring (PM10, PM2.5, SO2, NOx, CO)
  • DG Set & Boiler Flue Gas Stack Emission Testing
  • Drinking Water Analysis (IS 10500:2012 Complete Protocol)
  • Industrial Effluent (ETP) & Sewage (STP) Inlet/Outlet Analysis
  • Industrial DG Noise Monitoring & Workplace Acoustic Surveys
Standard: CPCB / IS 10500 / NAAQS View Full Scope
